# 1. Production North Star

Mục tiêu là tạo một thư viện media nguyên bản, có thể tái sử dụng cho Website, social, press, newspaper advertising, article/video covers, DEAL, SPACE, About, Homepage và future campaigns.

Anh Cao phải xuất hiện:

- credible
- calm
- observant
- human
- architectural
- consistent
- distinct across contexts

Nguyên tắc:

1. Người bình tĩnh; environment mang direction/orientation.
2. Không biến một buổi chụp thành một generic portrait session.
3. Mỗi media family có purpose, wardrobe, pose, context và reuse limit riêng.
4. Không staged documentary scene khiến người xem hiểu nhầm về client, listing, transaction, consultation, inspection, office hoặc team.
5. CONDITION inactive; không tạo Home Inspection promotional media.
6. Không dùng AI-generated replacement portrait.
7. Source phải đủ rộng và đủ độ phân giải để tạo desktop/mobile/social derivatives mà không phá crop.
8. Capture still và video riêng; không trích frame video làm portrait master trừ khi bất khả kháng.

# 6. Lighting System

## SETUP A — Green-Screen Portrait

Key: large soft source 45 degrees from camera, slightly above eye level.
Fill: soft reflector or low-output light 1–2 stops below key.
Separation: small rim/hair light from rear side if available.
Screen: two even lights aimed at screen, not subject.
White balance: lock around fixture’s actual color temperature; do not use auto if it shifts.
Contrast: medium; protect shadow detail in dark clothing.
Skin: no green spill; expose face correctly.
Common failure: subject too close to screen.
Correction: move subject 6–8 ft from screen, flag spill, rebalance screen lights.

## SETUP B — Neutral Press Portrait

Key: broad soft source near camera axis, slightly lateral.
Fill: reflector or low fill.
Background separation: one subtle hair/rim or background light.
Background: warm gray/mineral.
White balance: fixed.
Contrast: low-medium for print flexibility.
Common failure: flat passport look.
Correction: rotate shoulders, move key off-axis, increase background distance.

## SETUP C — Natural Window / Editorial Portrait

Key: window light from 30–60 degrees.
Fill: white reflector opposite.
Practical: one real lamp only if it belongs in the space and color matches.
White balance: set for daylight/window; avoid mixed green overhead lights.
Contrast: gentle.
Common failure: bright window and dark face.
Correction: turn body toward window, reduce background exposure or add reflector.

## SETUP D — Property Exterior

Key: sun used as back/side light when possible.
Fill: open shade, reflector or natural bounce.
Timing: early morning, late afternoon or bright overcast.
White balance: daylight locked.
Common failure: harsh midday eye shadows.
Correction: change angle, move to open shade, wait for softer light.

## SETUP E — Interior Spatial Context

Key: real window/door light where possible.
Fill: small soft LED bounced from wall/ceiling.
Practical: use existing warm lights only if color mix is controlled.
White balance: choose one dominant source.
Common failure: mixed orange/green skin.
Correction: switch off conflicting fixtures, gel/match light or move setup.

# 7. Green-Screen Setup

- Subject distance from screen: target 6–8 ft; minimum 4 ft only if space constrained.
- Screen illumination: even within roughly half a stop; no hot center.
- Subject illumination: completely separate from screen lights.
- Shadow avoidance: subject far enough from screen; key angled to throw shadow away.
- Spill avoidance: use distance, flags, rim light, matte wardrobe and lower screen brightness.
- Wardrobe restrictions: no green/mint/cyan; avoid reflective edges, fine patterns and translucent fabric.
- Hair-edge concerns: keep hair separated with rim light; avoid heavy flyaways unless intentional.
- Camera: standard main lens, not ultrawide; tripod; no digital zoom.
- Source resolution: highest practical still resolution; 4K for video if device allows.
- Exposure: lock focus/exposure/white balance; avoid auto exposure drift.
- Video shutter: natural motion; avoid very slow shutter blur. Capture 30fps master; 60fps only when slow motion is useful.
- Capture stills and video separately.
- Transparent PNG: derivative only from approved retouched master; never replace source master.
- Preserve original full-resolution files and clean-screen plates.
- Do not over-key edges; retain realistic hair/clothing texture.

# 8. Real-Location Rules

- Confirm permission for private property and intended public use.
- Avoid recognizable residents or bystanders unless authorized.
- Hide private documents, addresses, license plates and personal screens where unnecessary.
- Shoot from lawful/public positions where appropriate.
- Do not imply AC represents, listed, sold, inspected or consulted on a property unless true and authorized.
- Avoid third-party logos/signage unless contextually necessary and cleared.
- Record location, date, owner/permission source and intended use.
- Weather plan: primary date + backup date or indoor fallback.
- Capture wide establishing frame without address emphasis.
- Review drone, property release, street imagery and business-location usage with rights/compliance advisor where applicable.
- This document does not make legal conclusions.

# 9. Camera Framing System

## FULL BODY

Use: Homepage source, press layouts, transparent cutout.
Perspective: standard/portrait lens equivalent; avoid ultrawide distortion.
Distance: enough to keep body proportions natural.
Headroom: 8–15% safety.
Safe crop: include floor/shoes plus extra margin.
Common mistake: cutting feet or hands at edges.

## 3/4 BODY

Use: Homepage, service context, advertising.
Frame: mid-thigh to head with hand visibility.
Safety: capture wider alternate.
Common mistake: crop through hands/wrists.

## WAIST-UP

Use: About, editorial, mobile hero.
Frame: below waist/hips enough for 4:5 crop.
Common mistake: shoulders too square and stiff.

## HEAD-AND-SHOULDERS

Use: byline, profile, press, social avatar.
Frame: include upper chest and side safety.
Common mistake: crop too tight for 1:1 and 4:5 derivatives.

## HANDS / DETAIL

Use: Method, editorial, SPACE/DEAL detail.
Frame: include object and contextual surface.
Common mistake: hand without meaningful context.

## ENVIRONMENTAL WIDE

Use: DEAL/SPACE/About working context.
Frame: subject occupies roughly 20–40% depending purpose; environment remains legible.
Common mistake: environment overwhelms identity or implies false property relationship.

## WALKING / MOTION

Use: directional DEAL and social/video B-roll.
Frame: leave lead room.
Common mistake: walking too fast, motion blur, awkward mid-step.

# 22. File Naming

Master pattern:

ac-[family]-[page-or-context]-[role]-[orientation]-v[number]

## Naming rule

Public and Studio-facing filenames follow:

```text
ac-[family]-[page-or-context]-[role]-[orientation]-v[number]
```

Shot IDs such as `OWNER-01-A` remain in the production log, metadata and contact sheet. They do not replace the approved media-family vocabulary in the public filename.

## RAW / INGEST

Recommended pattern:

```text
ac-[family]-[context]-[role]-[orientation]-raw-[shot-id]-t[take]
```

Examples:

```text
ac-owner-home-hero-horizontal-raw-owner01a-t03
ac-owner-press-primary-vertical-raw-owner06a-t02
```

The original camera filename may be retained in embedded metadata or the ingest manifest, but the working ingest name must preserve the Shot ID and take number.

## SELECT

Recommended pattern:

```text
ac-[family]-[context]-[role]-[orientation]-select-v[number]
```

Examples:

```text
ac-owner-home-hero-horizontal-select-v01
ac-owner-space-threshold-wide-select-v01
```

## APPROVED MASTER

Recommended pattern:

```text
ac-[family]-[context]-[role]-[orientation]-master-v[number]
```

Examples:

```text
ac-owner-home-hero-horizontal-master-v01
ac-owner-about-primary-horizontal-master-v01
```

## DERIVATIVE

Recommended pattern:

```text
ac-[family]-[context]-[role]-[target-orientation-or-ratio]-v[number]
```

Examples:

```text
ac-owner-home-hero-mobile-4x5-v01
ac-owner-home-hero-desktop-3x2-v01
ac-owner-press-primary-social-1x1-v01
ac-owner-press-transparent-vertical-png-v01
```

## Language suffix

Add `-en` or `-vi` only when the exported visual itself contains localized text. Source portraits without baked-in text must not receive a language suffix.

Do not use:

final-final-2
IMG-random-number
new-newest
untitled-export

as public master names.
