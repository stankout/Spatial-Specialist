# 1. Advertising North Star

AC advertising should be recognizable before the viewer finishes reading it.

Not because it is loud.

Because it is clear.

Core proposition:

SEE THE WHOLE PROPERTY BEFORE MAKING THE MOVE.

Conceptual direction:

WHOLE-PROPERTY PERSPECTIVE

Required character:

- clear
- direct
- observant
- precise
- calm
- credible
- architectural
- human
- intelligent
- restrained

Core rules:

1. The message leads; visual systems support it.
2. Anh Cao remains human authority, not a synthetic mascot.
3. DEAL and SPACE remain distinct active perspectives.
4. A brand ad may orient to both perspectives; a service ad normally focuses on one.
5. Social share is not automatically advertising.
6. Open Graph is a preview, not a flyer.
7. Press/editorial profile graphics are not paid-response ads.
8. Every CTA leads to a truthful, published destination.
9. Every claim, credential, geographic statement and disclosure is managed/verified content.
10. CONDITION and Home Inspection remain absent from all public ad systems.

Avoid:

- luxury-Realtor cliché
- aggressive sales pressure
- motivational-speaker language
- mystical guru advertising
- technology-startup hype
- credential walls
- coupon aesthetics
- fake urgency or scarcity
- fake performance numbers
- generic “dream home” language
- exaggerated Feng Shui promise

# 2. Channel Taxonomy

## A. OPEN GRAPH / LINK PREVIEW

Purpose:
Identify the destination accurately when a public link is shared.

Characteristics:
- title-led
- destination-specific
- no QR
- no contact stack
- no aggressive CTA
- restrained environment
- 1.91:1 master

## B. ORGANIC SOCIAL POST GRAPHIC

Purpose:
Introduce an idea, service perspective, article, Guide, video or Owner viewpoint inside an ongoing content system.

Characteristics:
- educational/awareness tone
- can use 1:1, 4:5 or 9:16
- platform caption carries more detail
- graphic should not contain a full article summary

## C. PAID / PROMOTIONAL SOCIAL AD

Purpose:
Move a defined audience toward a truthful consultation, service or published-content destination.

Characteristics:
- one campaign objective
- one dominant CTA
- managed destination
- review/approval required
- paid-platform crop/safe-zone QA

## D. NEWSPAPER / COMMUNITY PUBLICATION AD

Purpose:
Introduce AC, DEAL or SPACE in a local print context with clear hierarchy, contact route and verified disclosure.

Characteristics:
- publication specifications control size/bleed/profile
- strong grayscale performance
- readable contact/QR
- no fragile low-contrast effects

## E. PRINT FLYER / HANDOUT

Purpose:
Provide a durable, controlled introduction to AC or one active service perspective.

Characteristics:
- may support more copy than a social ad
- one main action
- QR with visible destination
- contact/disclosure managed

## F. PRESS / EDITORIAL PROFILE GRAPHIC

Purpose:
Support an article, community profile, speaking/event mention or company introduction.

Characteristics:
- human/context-led
- Owner-06 primary
- no sales-pressure CTA by default
- attribution and publication context matter

## G. SERVICE AWARENESS CAMPAIGN

Purpose:
Explain DEAL or SPACE as a distinct perspective.

Characteristics:
- service name stable
- practical question-based message
- service-specific environment and Owner media where available

## H. CONTENT PROMOTION

Purpose:
Route to an Article, Guide or Video.

Characteristics:
- content title/category first
- no unrelated service pitch
- real cover/poster/author media only

## I. EVENT / COMMUNITY PLACEMENT

Purpose:
Support a verified event, publication, community announcement or profile placement.

Characteristics:
- date/time/location are managed data
- no fabricated event status
- rights and partner-logo review required

# 6. Social Share System

Social-share assets identify public destinations.

They are not required to contain:
- contact information
- QR
- multiple CTAs
- all services
- disclosures unrelated to destination

Master ratio:
1.91:1.

Conceptual working size:
1200 × 630.

Platform export size may change; maintain the master composition and current platform requirements.

Core anatomy:

1. AC identity
2. destination title
3. service/content identifier
4. optional media
5. restrained environment
6. safe margins

## Text limits

Design targets, not copy-authoring permission:

- Title: target 35–70 characters; maximum 3 lines.
- Descriptor: target 20–45 characters; 1–2 lines.
- Category/service: 1 short line.
- Optional author: one short line.

When title is too long:
- preserve full SEO/social title in metadata where supported
- use approved shorter display title only if content governance provides it
- otherwise reduce type scale within the approved minimum and allow three lines
- never silently rewrite or truncate into a misleading title

## Fallback hierarchy

1. approved content/route-specific graphic
2. approved category/service template
3. approved global AC share card

No broken blank media area.

# 12. Newsprint Rules

Newsprint is less forgiving than screen or coated print.

## Contrast

- Use strong tonal separation.
- Avoid dark-on-dark green distinctions.
- Keep body/support text on calm, high-contrast field.
- Do not depend on subtle gradients to separate content.

## Type

Design targets unless publication specifies otherwise:
- avoid body/disclosure text below approximately 9 pt
- use larger minimum for low-quality community newsprint
- avoid hairline weights
- avoid extremely condensed faces for Vietnamese

Publication proof controls final minimum.

## Lines and geometry

- avoid ultra-thin datum lines
- test at final printed size
- simplify environments into fewer, thicker structural cues
- do not print dense procedural noise

## Dark coverage

Create ink-conscious light and dark variants where necessary.

Full dark-field ads require printer proof and should not be default for low-quality newsprint.

## Grayscale

Every newspaper layout must pass grayscale proof.

DEAL and SPACE cannot rely on brass vs mint alone; labels and geometry must distinguish them.

## QR

Use strong white/quiet zone, high contrast and verified physical scan at final size.

## Proofing

Required:
- on-screen grayscale proof
- physical-size print proof
- printer/publication proof where available

# 13. Print Color Modes

## SCREEN / DIGITAL

- RGB
- sRGB-oriented delivery unless platform requires otherwise
- embedded color profile where supported

## COMMERCIAL PRINT

- CMYK using printer-specified ICC/profile
- publication profile wins
- soft-proof before export
- rich black only according to printer guidance
- small black text should use printer-safe black build

## NEWSPRINT COLOR

- use publication-specified CMYK/newsprint profile
- lower total ink coverage according to printer specification
- simplify gradients and shadow detail

## GRAYSCALE / ONE-COLOR

- create deliberate grayscale layout
- do not rely on automated conversion only
- retain service labels and hierarchy

## SPOT COLOR

Optional only when printer/publication supports it and cost is approved.

Do not invent Pantone requirements without a print-production decision.
