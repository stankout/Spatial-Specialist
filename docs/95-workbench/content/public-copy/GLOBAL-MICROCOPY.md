| Purpose | English | Vietnamese | When to use | Do not use for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Main service gateway | Explore Services | Khám phá Dịch vụ | Homepage orientation | Deep-page specific action |
| DEAL destination | Explore DEAL | Khám phá DEAL | Routing to DEAL | Generic contact |
| SPACE destination | Explore SPACE | Khám phá SPACE | Routing to SPACE | Generic contact |
| Consultation request | Request a Consultation | Yêu cầu Tư vấn | Consultation / booking entry | Confirmed appointment |
| DEAL inquiry | Contact About a Property | Liên hệ về Bất động sản | Property-specific inquiry | SPACE inquiry |
| General contact | Contact AC | Liên hệ AC | General communication | Confirming a booking |
| Form inquiry | Submit Inquiry | Gửi Yêu cầu | Contact forms | Booking confirmation |
| Booking request | Submit Request | Gửi Yêu cầu | Booking/request workflow | “Appointment booked” state |
| Insights hub | Explore Insights | Khám phá Kiến thức | Content discovery | Service pages |
| Article | Read | Đọc | Text content | Video |
| Video | Watch | Xem | Video content | Article |
| Search | Search | Tìm kiếm | Search action | Browse navigation |
| Back navigation | Return | Quay lại | Returning to prior context | Primary conversion |
| Error recovery | Try Again | Thử lại | Recoverable error | Normal CTA |

## CTA Rules

- Prefer verb + destination or verb + action.
- Avoid repeating “Learn More” across unrelated destinations.
- Do not use urgency unless a real, verifiable time condition exists.
- Do not use “Book Now” when the workflow only submits a request.
- A page should normally have one dominant action, with secondary paths clearly subordinate.

# VOICE PERSON MODEL

## AC VOICE

Use AC as the speaker when explaining:

- company purpose;
- service approach;
- methodology;
- public analysis;
- boundaries;
- recommendations made by the AC system.

Preferred:

> AC examines the transaction context before recommending a next step.

> AC separates observation from interpretation.

Vietnamese:

> AC xem xét bối cảnh giao dịch trước khi đề xuất bước tiếp theo.

> AC tách rõ quan sát khỏi diễn giải.

### Default rule

Use “AC,” not “we,” for company-level public copy.

This keeps the system/company voice distinct and prevents accidental claims about team size or organizational structure.

---

## ANH CAO VOICE

Use first person only when Anh Cao is explicitly presented as the speaker, author, or practitioner.

Preferred:

> I look at the context before I make a recommendation.

> I want the reader to understand what is observed, what is interpreted, and what remains uncertain.

Vietnamese:

> Tôi xem xét bối cảnh trước khi đưa ra đề xuất.

> Tôi muốn người đọc hiểu điều gì được quan sát, điều gì là diễn giải và điều gì vẫn còn chưa chắc chắn.

Do not switch casually between:

I / we / AC.

A quoted or authored section may use “I.”

Normal company copy uses “AC.”

---

## CTA VOICE

CTA language should be:

- short;
- verb-led;
- specific;
- calm;
- destination-aware.

Preferred:

- Explore DEAL
- Request a Consultation
- Contact AC
- Read
- Watch

Avoid:

- Unlock Your Future
- Transform Your Property Today
- Don’t Miss Out
- Get Started Now

unless a future offer genuinely requires and supports such language.

---

## LEGAL / SYSTEM VOICE

Use literal, neutral language.

No brand flourish.

Examples:

> This field is required.

> Your request has been received.

> This content is temporarily unavailable.

Vietnamese:

> Trường này là bắt buộc.

> Yêu cầu của bạn đã được ghi nhận.

> Nội dung này đang tạm thời không khả dụng.

# GLOBAL COPY RULES

1. Introduce the full brand before relying on “AC” where context is not already established.
2. Keep DEAL and SPACE uppercase as system names.
3. Translate their descriptors, not the identifiers.
4. Do not use “expert,” “licensed,” “certified,” “award-winning,” “proven,” or scale claims unless separately verified and approved.
5. Do not turn market context into a prediction.
6. Do not turn spatial interpretation into scientific fact.
7. Do not imply that one active perspective requires the other.
8. Do not promote future products simply because supporting architecture exists.
9. Prefer specific verbs and nouns over marketing adjectives.
10. When uncertain, say what is uncertain.
