Exploratory architecture or proposal.

Not canonical.

Example:

SPEC-001-AC-Knowledge-Architecture.md

---

## MASTER DRAFT

Candidate for company-level canonical truth after owner review.

Example:

K1-AC-MASTER-BRIEF-DRAFT.md

---

## DOMAIN DRAFT

Candidate canonical definition inside a domain.

Examples:

Brand

Business

Design

Operations

Data

Products

---

## TEMPLATE

Reusable document structure.

Templates do not define current business truth.

---

## OWNER-DECISION STAGING

Contains unresolved questions requiring Owner determination.

Not canonical until decisions are resolved into the relevant canonical documents.

---

## MIGRATION CONTROL

Temporary documents used to organize Knowledge Architecture V1 migration.

K8 belongs here.

# 15. Canonicalization Principle

Migration does not mean every file becomes approved company truth.

Codex must preserve lifecycle distinctions.

Migration may create:

CANONICAL

DRAFT

PROPOSED

TEMPLATE

NON-CANONICAL

documents in their intended architecture.

Where an unresolved Owner decision affects a document, preserve the unresolved marker rather than inventing an answer.

---

# 16. Critical Invariants

During migration:

DEAL remains ACTIVE.

SPACE remains ACTIVE.

CONDITION remains INACTIVE.

Draft is not Published.

Reference is not Production Asset.

Product proposal is not Product activation.

Market Pulse methodology is not validated merely because it is documented.

Production providers remain governed by actual current readiness.

Codex must not commit, push, deploy, activate services, or enable production providers unless explicitly authorized.
