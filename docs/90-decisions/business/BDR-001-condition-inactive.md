# BDR-001 — CONDITION remains inactive

Status: CURRENT POLICY
Domain: Business
Effective date: Not asserted by K0

## Decision

CONDITION is defined for internal architecture but remains INACTIVE and publicly hidden. It cannot appear as an available service in navigation, service listings, booking, public CTAs, public content discovery, or sitemap output.

## Reason

Technical capability is not service activation. Activation requires an explicit Owner decision and a complete readiness/compliance review.
