# PDR-002 — Assignment is not Approval

Status: CURRENT POLICY
Domain: Platform
Effective date: Not asserted by K0

## Decision

Assigning media to a slot does not grant public-use approval. Import, privacy, rights, metadata, compatibility, and explicit public-use gates remain separate.

## Reason

Placement intent cannot establish ownership, permission, privacy safety, or public readiness.

See [Media Library](../../MEDIA_LIBRARY.md) and [Media Direction](../../30-design/MEDIA_DIRECTION.md).
