# PDR-001 — Draft is not Published

Status: CURRENT POLICY
Domain: Platform
Effective date: Not asserted by K0

## Decision

Editing or saving a Draft does not change normal public output. Publication is a separate authorized transition, and locale/service eligibility still applies.

## Reason

This protects Owner review, prevents accidental release, and keeps preview behavior distinct from production behavior.

See [Publishing Model](../../80-platform/PUBLISHING_MODEL.md).
