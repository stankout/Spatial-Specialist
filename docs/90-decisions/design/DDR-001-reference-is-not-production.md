# DDR-001 — Reference is not Production Asset

Status: CURRENT POLICY
Domain: Design
Effective date: Not asserted by K0

## Decision

Reference material is research or inspiration only. It may become production media only after rights, privacy, quality, provenance, and Owner approval are established through the media workflow.

## Reason

Public availability does not grant copying or publication rights, and visual inspiration must not silently enter production.

See [Reference Governance](../../../references/README.md).
