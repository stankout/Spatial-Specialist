# AC Spatial Specialist — Voice and Tone

Status: DRAFT
Document Class: Brand Communication Specification

---

## 1. Core Voice

AC communicates with:

CLARITY
DIRECTNESS
CURIOSITY
PRECISION
HUMAN CONTEXT

The company should sound informed without pretending to know more than it does.

---

## 2. Core Communication Principle

No unnecessary sugarcoating.

No unnecessary complexity.

No unsupported certainty.

The reader should understand:

what we know
what we observe
what we interpret
what we recommend
and what remains uncertain.

---

## 3. Preferred Style

Use:

- clear language
- concrete examples
- organized reasoning
- direct conclusions
- useful context
- specific terminology when needed
- explanation of technical terms
- confident but bounded statements

Avoid:

- excessive marketing adjectives
- empty luxury language
- fake urgency
- unexplained jargon
- mystical certainty
- exaggerated guarantees
- unnecessary corporate language

---

## 4. Website Tone

Website communication should be:

- composed
- precise
- confident
- modern
- concise where possible
- explanatory where necessary

The website may have a sophisticated technological visual identity while the writing remains easy to understand.

---

## 5. Social Media Tone

Social content may be:

- more conversational
- more energetic
- more personal
- provocative when supported by reasoning
- humorous when appropriate

However:

strong personality must not convert unsupported opinion into apparent fact.

---

## 6. Educational Content Tone

Educational content should:

- introduce the question
- explain the context
- separate fact from interpretation
- show reasoning
- identify limitations
- provide useful next actions

The objective is understanding, not merely authority.

---

## 7. Market Intelligence Tone

Market reports should distinguish:

OBSERVATION

Example:
Mortgage rates increased over the measured period.

INTERPRETATION

Example:
Higher financing costs may reduce purchasing power.

IMPLICATION

Example:
Some buyers may gain negotiating leverage if demand weakens.

Do not convert correlation or directional context into certainty without evidence.

---

## 8. SPACE / Feng Shui Tone

When discussing Feng Shui or other interpretive spatial frameworks:

state observations clearly.

Separate:

observable physical conditions

from:

interpretive framework.

Avoid presenting metaphysical interpretations as established scientific fact.

The content may still discuss traditional concepts accurately within their own framework.

---

## 9. Client Communication Tone

Client communication should be:

- respectful
- direct
- helpful
- organized
- calm

Explain problems without humiliating the client.

Explain uncertainty instead of hiding it.

Avoid artificial pressure.

---

## 10. Advertising Tone

Advertising should communicate:

- problem
- perspective
- value
- next step

It should not depend on:

- exaggerated claims
- false scarcity
- fake success metrics
- guaranteed outcomes

---

## 11. Internal Management Tone

Internal communication should prioritize:

- ownership
- clarity
- action
- deadlines
- evidence
- accountability

Instructions should state:

WHAT
WHY when relevant
WHO
WHEN
EXPECTED RESULT.

---

## 12. English and Vietnamese

English and Vietnamese are first-class communication languages.

Translations should preserve:

meaning
tone
intent
professional boundaries

rather than translate word-for-word where that produces unnatural language.

Neither language should be treated as a secondary machine-translated version of the other.

---

## 13. Brand Phrases

Potential philosophy language may include:

No BS.
No Sugarcoat.
Straight facts.
Clear perspective.
Understand before you decide.

These phrases are brand-language candidates, not mandatory slogans.

Use should depend on context.

Formal advertising or professional documentation may require more restrained wording.

---

## 14. Claims Standard

Before making a public factual claim, ask:

Is it true?

Can AC support it?

Is the relevant context missing?

Could a reasonable reader interpret it more broadly than intended?

If a claim cannot survive those questions, rewrite it.
