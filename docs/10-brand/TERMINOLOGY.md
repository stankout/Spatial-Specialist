## AC

Preferred shorthand for AC Spatial Specialist.

Use after brand context is established.

Primary public brand.

Legal entity relationship:
TBD — owner verification required.

## DEAL

AC service perspective focused on Real Estate.

Status:
ACTIVE

DEAL should not be used as a generic synonym for every transaction or product.

---

## SPACE

AC service perspective focused on Spatial Consultation.

Status:
ACTIVE

SPACE may include Feng Shui interpretation, but SPACE is broader than Feng Shui alone.

---

## CONDITION

Future AC service perspective focused on physical property condition / Home Inspection.

Status:
INACTIVE

Do not present as available public service.

---

## Service

A professional or business activity AC offers to a customer.

Examples:

Real Estate service
Spatial Consultation

A software feature is not automatically a Service.

---

## Product

A defined item, application, digital good, physical good, or paid experience provided through the AC ecosystem.

Examples may include:

AC Market Pulse
reports
courses
digital goods
merchandise

---

## Feature

A capability of the website/platform.

Examples:

search
Draft/Publish
media assignment
social-channel management

A Feature does not automatically represent a Product or Service.

---

## Platform

The AC digital system comprising public experiences and internal operational capabilities.

---

## Studio

The private/admin-side AC platform used to manage company digital operations.

---

## Draft

A working state that is not automatically public.

Draft state may be edited or previewed before publication.

---

## Published

The approved state used by normal public-facing experiences.

Published does not necessarily mean legally permanent or irreversible.

---

## Reference

External or internal material used for research, inspiration, analysis, or comparison.

Reference status does not grant production-use permission.

---

## Production Asset

Media or material approved for actual public or operational use.

---

## Owner Media

Media belonging to or provided by the company owner, subject to appropriate usage and privacy rules.

---

## Procedural Scene

An original code-generated visual environment created from AC visual-system primitives.

Examples:

AC Ambient Intelligence
Market Network
Spatial Field

---

## Market Intelligence

The process and products used to organize, analyze, contextualize, and explain market-related information.

---

## Raw Data

A sourced observation before AC-specific scoring or interpretation is applied.

---

## Derived Indicator

A value calculated from one or more data inputs according to a documented methodology.

---

## Interpretation

AC's explanation of what observed information may mean.

Interpretation must not be presented as raw fact.

---

## AC Market Pulse

Working product name for a future market-intelligence system that summarizes multiple market conditions into an understandable market climate.

Status:
PROPOSED

Canonical product specification does not yet exist.

---

## Lead

A person or organization that may become a client/customer and has entered an AC inquiry or acquisition workflow.

---

## Client

A person or organization engaged in a service relationship where that terminology is appropriate.

---

## Customer

A person or organization purchasing or using an AC product where customer terminology is appropriate.

Client and Customer are not automatically interchangeable.

---

## Owner

The company owner and final authority for company-level decisions defined by AC governance.

---

## Manager

A delegated operational role responsible for approved areas of day-to-day company operations.

Manager authority does not automatically include company strategy or unrestricted publication authority.

---

## Content Editor

A role focused on content creation and editing within granted permissions.

---

## Developer

A technical role responsible for software implementation and technical systems within granted authority.

Developer implementation does not redefine business policy.

---

## Active

Currently approved and available for its intended operational/public use.

---

## Inactive

Defined but deliberately unavailable.

---

## Future

Intended or possible later capability that is not currently active.

---

## Proposed

Defined sufficiently for evaluation but not yet approved as active implementation truth.

---

## Canonical

The authoritative definition within the scope assigned to a document.

---

## Workbench

Repository area for non-canonical ideas, research, experiments, and proposals.

---

## Archive

Historical material that is retained but no longer authoritative.
