# AC Data Indicator — CPI-U Year-over-Year Inflation

Status: DRAFT
Indicator ID: IND-CPI-YOY
Document Class: Data Indicator Specification
Intended Canonical Location: docs/60-data/indicators/CPI_YOY.md

---

## 1. Definition

The year-over-year percent change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, All Items, U.S. city average.

Base source series:

CPI-U
All Items
U.S. city average
Not seasonally adjusted

---

## 2. Primary Source

Source ID:

SOURCE-BLS

Provider:

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics

Series ID:

CUUR0000SA0

---

## 3. Unit

Percent change from the same month one year earlier.

---

## 4. Raw Source Value

Underlying CPI index.

The YoY rate may be:

- retrieved from the official publication
or
- calculated from the current index and index 12 months earlier

if methodology is equivalent and documented.

---

## 5. Frequency

MONTHLY

---

## 6. Geography

U.S. city average.

National inflation context.

Not a local Georgia housing-cost index.

---

## 7. Seasonal Adjustment

For canonical CPI YoY:

use the NOT SEASONALLY ADJUSTED CPI-U All Items series.

This allows like-month year-over-year comparison.

---

## 8. Derived Values

Potential:

YoY %

3-month trend

change in YoY inflation rate

Do not casually annualize monthly CPI changes and label them CPI YoY.

---

## 9. Direction

For MACRO PRESSURE:

Higher inflation
→ potentially greater inflation pressure.

But housing impact is CONTEXT DEPENDENT.

Inflation alone does not determine:

mortgage rates
Fed action
house prices
buyer leverage.

## 12. Interpretation

Preferred wording:

"Consumer prices were X% higher than the same month one year earlier."

Avoid:

"Everything became X% more expensive."

---

## 13. Limitations

CPI is a broad consumer-price index.

It does not measure only housing-market prices.

Do not substitute:

home-price inflation

for:

CPI inflation.

---

## 14. DO NOT

Do not confuse:

CPI-U

Core CPI

PCE

Core PCE

Home Price Index

They are different measures.
