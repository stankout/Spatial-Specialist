# AC Spatial Specialist — Manager Handbook

Status: DRAFT
Document Class: Operational Handbook
Intended Canonical Location: docs/70-operations/MANAGER_HANDBOOK.md

---

## 1. Role

The Manager helps convert Owner direction into reliable daily operation.

The Manager should protect:

- priorities
- workflow
- quality
- communication
- deadlines
- records
- escalation

The Manager is not expected to independently redefine the company.

---

## 2. Daily Operating View

The Manager should be able to understand:

WHAT NEEDS ATTENTION?

WHO OWNS IT?

WHEN IS IT DUE?

WHAT IS BLOCKED?

WHAT NEEDS APPROVAL?

WHAT AFFECTS A CUSTOMER?

---

## 3. Core Queues

Potential operational queues include:

- new leads
- bookings
- customer follow-ups
- active service work
- content drafts
- media review
- publishing
- unresolved issues
- staff tasks
- platform issues

Studio should eventually surface these clearly.

---

## 4. Priority Order

As a default:

1. urgent customer/safety/security issues
2. commitments already made to customers
3. time-sensitive transactions or appointments
4. blocked work
5. scheduled content/operations
6. optimization and future work

Actual priorities may vary by situation.

---

## 5. Delegation

When delegating work, specify:

WHAT
WHO
DUE
EXPECTED RESULT
APPROVAL LEVEL

Avoid ambiguous assignment such as:

“Take care of the website.”

---

## 6. Escalate to Owner When

Examples:

- company direction changes
- new service/product activation
- major pricing decision
- credential issue
- significant customer dispute
- major public claim
- legal/compliance uncertainty
- major financial commitment
- authority conflict

---

## 7. Escalate to Developer When

Examples:

- application error
- permission issue
- data integrity issue
- failed integration
- deployment issue
- security concern
- technical performance problem

Business questions should not be disguised as technical bugs.

---

## 8. Content Oversight

Manager responsibilities may include:

- pipeline health
- scheduling
- completeness
- required review
- publishing readiness

The Manager should verify that content respects current:

- service status
- brand voice
- claims standards
- media approval
- language requirements

---

## 9. Customer Oversight

The Manager should prevent leads/customers from disappearing into an unowned queue.

Every actionable customer record should eventually have:

OWNER
STATUS
NEXT ACTION

where the operational system supports those fields.

---

## 10. Reporting

A useful management report should focus on exceptions and decisions rather than dumping every activity.

Examples:

- leads needing action
- overdue tasks
- bookings
- content awaiting approval
- customer issues
- technical blockers
- decisions needed from Owner

---

## 11. Improvement

When the same operational problem appears repeatedly:

Do not normalize the problem.

Identify whether AC needs:

- clearer responsibility
- better interface
- automation
- SOP
- training
- changed policy

Repeated manual confusion is evidence that the operating system needs improvement.
