# AC Spatial Specialist — Approval Model

Status: DRAFT
Document Class: Company Governance Specification
Intended Canonical Location: docs/70-operations/APPROVAL_MODEL.md

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines when work may proceed directly and when review or Owner approval is required.

The objective is to protect important decisions without forcing every routine action through the Owner.

---

## 2. Approval Levels

### A0 — ROUTINE EXECUTION

No special approval required beyond existing authority.

Examples:

- organizing approved media
- editing a draft
- routine record maintenance
- scheduling approved content

---

### A1 — REVIEW REQUIRED

Work may be prepared by delegated staff but requires review before final use.

Examples:

- new page copy
- advertising creative
- new article
- public educational material
- significant visual changes

Reviewer may be Manager or Owner depending on scope.

---

### A2 — CONTROLLED APPROVAL

Requires designated authority before execution or publication.

Examples:

- pricing change
- significant public business claim
- customer-facing workflow change
- major campaign
- production configuration change
- sensitive data access change

Usually Manager + Owner or Owner depending on the decision.

---

### A3 — OWNER DECISION

Owner approval required.

Examples:

- new service
- service activation/deactivation
- major company positioning
- major product launch
- credential-sensitive claims
- hiring/termination
- major financial commitment
- production go-live
- major legal/compliance policy
- company authority changes

---

## 3. Content Approval

Typical content workflow:

IDEA
→ DRAFT
→ REVIEW
→ APPROVED
→ PUBLISHED

Routine updates to already approved factual information may follow a lighter workflow where authority has been delegated.

---

## 4. Media Approval

External/imported media:

IMPORT
→ RIGHTS / PRIVACY REVIEW
→ METADATA
→ APPROVAL
→ ASSIGNMENT
→ PUBLISH

Reference-only media must never advance automatically into production use.

---

## 5. Product Approval

IDEA
→ PROPOSAL
→ PRODUCT SPEC
→ OWNER APPROVAL
→ BUILD
→ QA
→ ACTIVATION

Building a prototype does not automatically approve the product.

---

## 6. Service Approval

PROPOSAL
→ SERVICE SPEC
→ READINESS REVIEW
→ OWNER DECISION
→ OPERATIONAL PREPARATION
→ PUBLIC ACTIVATION

Service activation is always an explicit event.

---

## 7. Technical Approval

Technical implementation that does not alter company behavior may proceed within Developer authority.

Changes involving:

- public business behavior
- permissions
- production activation
- customer data
- payment
- service availability
- strategic integrations

require the corresponding business approval.

---

## 8. Emergency Actions

Where immediate action is needed to protect:

- customer information
- security
- data integrity
- public safety
- platform integrity

authorized technical personnel may take the minimum reasonable protective action.

Material emergency actions should be documented and reported to Owner afterward.

---

## 9. Approval Evidence

Important approvals should become discoverable through one or more appropriate records:

- decision record
- Studio audit record
- product/service status
- approved specification
- operational record

Chat messages alone should not be the durable evidence of major company decisions.
