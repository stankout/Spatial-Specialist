# AC Spatial Specialist — Motion Language

Status: DRAFT
Document Class: Design Specification
Intended Canonical Location: docs/30-design/MOTION_LANGUAGE.md

---

## 1. Motion Principle

Motion should make the digital environment feel alive without competing with information.

Bad motion:

Everything moves.

Good motion:

The environment appears active beneath a controlled interface.

---

## 2. Motion Hierarchy

### ENVIRONMENT

Slow ambient movement.

### SECTION

Occasional transition or spatial change.

### CONTENT

Reveal when useful.

### INTERACTION

Immediate response to hover, focus or user action.

### CTA

Controlled signal/light response.

---

## 3. Suitable Motion Vocabulary

AC may use:

- slow ambient gradient drift
- technical grid drift
- signal sweep
- scan line
- node/network motion
- subtle radar
- light pass
- controlled glow pulse
- parallax
- section reveal
- procedural field movement

---

## 4. Avoid

Avoid:

- fast continuous movement
- random particles everywhere
- large objects constantly floating
- aggressive glitch
- repeated flicker
- constant CTA animation
- decorative movement with no hierarchy

---

## 5. Motion by Brand Perspective

### DEAL

Directional.

Strategic.

Network-oriented.

Possible behaviors:

- lateral signal
- market network
- geographic connection
- directional lines

### SPACE

Spatial.

Environmental.

Possible behaviors:

- field movement
- scan
- orientation arc
- slow geometry
- circulation signal

### CONTENT

Quiet editorial movement.

Possible behaviors:

- reveal
- image response
- metadata illumination
- subtle archival motion

---

## 6. Background Motion

Background video or procedural scenes should:

- remain readable beneath foreground content
- avoid hard cuts when looping
- avoid high brightness changes
- avoid fake data
- provide reduced-motion fallback

---

## 7. Reduced Motion

Reduced-motion mode should remove or simplify:

- parallax
- continuous scans
- flicker
- animated gradients
- background video
- unnecessary reveals

Interaction state changes must remain understandable.

---

## 8. Mobile Motion

Mobile should use lower:

- node density
- grid density
- parallax
- blur
- decorative complexity

Motion complexity should adapt, not merely scale down.

---

## 9. Purpose Test

Before adding motion, ask:

What relationship does this movement communicate?

If the answer is only:

"It looks futuristic."

simplify or remove it.
