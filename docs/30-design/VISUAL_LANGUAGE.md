# AC Spatial Specialist — Visual Language

Status: DRAFT
Document Class: Design Specification
Intended Canonical Location: docs/30-design/VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md

---

## 1. Core Direction

AC visual identity:

ARCHITECTURAL CYBER EDITORIAL

The visual system combines:

ARCHITECTURE
+
SPACE
+
PROPERTY
+
INFORMATION
+
TECHNOLOGY
+
EDITORIAL COMPOSITION

---

## 2. Desired Experience

AC should feel:

- intelligent
- spatial
- cinematic
- technical
- premium
- calm
- precise
- mysterious without becoming obscure
- futuristic without becoming childish

---

## 3. Avoid

Avoid:

- generic SaaS design
- repeated identical cards
- giant flat white sections
- casino neon
- excessive purple cyberpunk
- arcade/gaming clutter
- animation everywhere
- unreadable glass interfaces
- fake dashboards
- arbitrary futuristic decoration

---

## 4. Composition

Prefer varied composition:

- asymmetric split
- cinematic hero
- editorial rail
- full-bleed environment
- technical pathway
- structured vertical progression
- spatial circuit
- information terminal
- data rail

Not every section should be centered.

Not every concept should become a card.

---

## 5. Depth

Interfaces may use:

- atmospheric backgrounds
- glass
- controlled translucency
- layered surfaces
- edge illumination
- ambient tint
- restrained shadows
- controlled blur

Foreground and background should feel related.

Avoid opaque panels appearing unrelated to the visual environment.

---

## 6. Geometry

Useful visual language:

- architectural guides
- grids
- circuits
- connection lines
- direction markers
- measurement-like details
- indexed nodes
- technical framing

Geometry should support composition.

It should not become decorative noise.

---

## 7. Typography

Typography should distinguish:

DISPLAY

PAGE TITLE

SECTION TITLE

BODY

TECHNICAL LABEL

METADATA

CTA

MICRO LABEL

Display typography may be expressive and technological.

Long-form text must remain highly readable.

Vietnamese typography must receive equal consideration.

---

## 8. Color

Primary visual environment may use:

- black
- deep green
- emerald
- mint
- dark blue
- restrained cyan

Contextual accents may include:

- brass
- gold
- violet
- neutral warm tones

Color should have semantic purpose.

Do not distribute every brand accent across every screen.

---

## 9. DEAL Identity

DEAL should suggest:

- market
- geography
- transaction
- decision
- intelligence
- direction

Potential language:

dark green
+
brass
+
network
+
directional geometry.

---

## 10. SPACE Identity

SPACE should suggest:

- orientation
- circulation
- field
- geometry
- relationship
- spatial interpretation

Potential language:

dark spatial field
+
emerald
+
mint
+
cyan
+
architectural geometry.

---

## 11. CONDITION Identity

CONDITION may eventually develop its own identity after service activation planning.

Do not create public CONDITION visual marketing while service is inactive.

---

## 12. Human Presence

Portraits should feel integrated into environments rather than arbitrarily boxed.

Human subjects should remain visually dominant when the page is about identity/trust.

Technology should support the person rather than bury them.

---

## 13. Visual Honesty

Decorative data visuals must remain decorative.

Do not use fake:

- charts
- market numbers
- property statistics
- performance metrics

in a way that appears factual.

---

## 14. Responsive Principle

Desktop may use:

- depth
- asymmetry
- spatial composition

Mobile should transform into:

- strong sequence
- clear hierarchy
- simplified geometry
- lighter motion

Do not simply shrink desktop design.
