## 1. Advertising Objective

AC advertising should introduce:

WHO

WHAT PERSPECTIVE AC PROVIDES

WHY IT MAY MATTER

WHAT THE AUDIENCE SHOULD DO NEXT

Advertising should not attempt to explain the entire company at once.

## 4. Newspaper Advertising

Newspaper design should prioritize:

- readable hierarchy
- strong black/white reproduction
- restrained visual complexity
- high-resolution print output
- clear contact information

Before final production, verify:

- publication dimensions
- bleed
- color mode
- DPI
- file format
- advertising requirements
- applicable professional disclosure requirements

Do not create one arbitrary "newspaper size."

---

## 5. Digital Advertising

Master creative may produce variants for:

- 1:1
- 4:5
- 9:16
- 16:9

Design should adapt composition rather than crop blindly.

---

## 6. Advertising Hierarchy

Recommended structure:

BRAND
↓
PERSON / SUBJECT
↓
VALUE / PERSPECTIVE
↓
SERVICE
↓
CTA
↓
CONTACT / DISCLOSURE

---

## 7. Claims

Advertising must not invent:

- sales volume
- rankings
- awards
- testimonials
- credentials
- guaranteed results
- artificial urgency

Use verified facts only.

---

## 8. Visual Tone

Advertising may use:

- cinematic portrait
- architectural geometry
- technical framing
- subtle data/spatial elements
- premium typography
- controlled AC green/mint accents

The subject and message must remain more important than decoration.

---

## 9. AC Advertising System

Long-term, one approved master creative direction should support:

PRINT
→ newspaper / flyer

SOCIAL
→ square / portrait / story

WEB
→ About / campaign landing

CONTACT
→ digital contact card

This allows a consistent identity without reproducing the exact same composition in every format.
