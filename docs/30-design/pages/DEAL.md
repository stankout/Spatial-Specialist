---

## 1. Purpose

Introduce AC's real-estate perspective and organize users toward an appropriate transaction/property decision path.

---

## 2. User Questions

How can AC help with my property decision?

Am I buying, selling or evaluating?

What factors matter?

What should I do next?

---

## 3. Primary Pathways

BUY

SELL

INVEST / PROPERTY DECISION where applicable

---

## 4. Visual Identity

MARKET INTELLIGENCE
+
PROPERTY CONTEXT
+
TRANSACTION STRATEGY

Potential visual language:

- deep green
- brass accents
- directional geometry
- Market Network
- geographic rhythm
- technical indexing

---

## 5. Pathway Design

Avoid three generic feature cards.

Prefer:

- tactical rail
- decision branches
- indexed pathways
- connected modules
- directional flow

---

## 6. Market Context

Market intelligence may support DEAL.

Never fabricate live market numbers.

Real data must have appropriate source/context/freshness.

---

## 7. Geographic Context

Local/regional presentation may eventually include geographic operating context.

Do not imply service coverage outside verified current business ability.

---

## 8. Relationship to SPACE

SPACE may be offered independently as another perspective.

No forced bundle.

---

## 9. Relationship to CONDITION

CONDITION remains unavailable.

Do not present AC Home Inspection as a DEAL add-on while inactive.

---

## 10. Motion

Directional movement.

Network rhythm.

Subtle signal.

Avoid stock-trading-terminal chaos.

---

## 11. Mobile

Convert broad tactical layouts into clear sequential decisions.

---

## 12. Primary CTA

Should correspond to the user's DEAL context.

Examples:

start buyer conversation
start seller conversation
contact regarding property

Final labels remain content/owner decisions.

---

## 13. DO NOT

Do not use:

- fake listings
- fake market dashboards
- fake transaction statistics
- guaranteed property outcomes
- invented brokerage facts
