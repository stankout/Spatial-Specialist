# AC Spatial Specialist — CONTACT Page Specification

Status: DRAFT
Page ID: CONTACT
Document Class: Public Page Specification
Intended Canonical Location: docs/30-design/pages/CONTACT.md

---

## 1. Purpose

Help users connect with the correct AC perspective without ambiguity.

---

## 2. Core Concept

Connect with the right perspective.

The existing contact/footer architecture is considered successful and should not be destructively redesigned.

---

## 3. Contact Paths

REAL ESTATE

and

SPATIAL SPECIALIST

should remain clearly distinguishable where current business/contact architecture requires it.

---

## 4. Primary Question

"What do you need help with?"

Then route toward the appropriate contact/service context.

---

## 5. Social

Published social channels should come from the centralized Published social collection.

Do not hardcode another independent social list.

---

## 6. Visual Identity

Quiet final architectural environment.

Strong hierarchy.

Low motion.

High trust.

---

## 7. Forms

Where forms exist:

- clear labels
- visible validation
- keyboard accessibility
- clear service choice
- appropriate consent
- no decorative HUD behavior that harms usability

---

## 8. CONDITION

Must not be selectable as an available service while inactive.

---

## 9. Responsive

Contact information must remain easy to read/copy/tap on mobile.

---

## 10. DO NOT

Do not:

- bury contact information beneath animation
- merge distinct business contexts ambiguously
- create duplicate social configuration
- destructively redesign approved contact composition
