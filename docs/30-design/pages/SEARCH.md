Status: DRAFT

---

## 1. Purpose

Help users discover AC information across services, content and future products.

---

## 2. Primary Interaction

SEARCH

Interaction must remain immediately understandable.

Visual style must not interfere with conventional search behavior.

---

## 3. Visual Identity

INFORMATION TERMINAL

Potential scene:

Signal Terminal
or
Global Intelligence

---

## 4. Results

Results should clearly expose useful metadata such as:

type
topic
service relationship
language where relevant

Avoid visually treating all results as identical generic cards.

---

## 5. Empty State

Explain:

- no matching results
- possible next action
- useful navigation

Do not leave an empty terminal-looking screen with no explanation.

---

## 6. Future Scope

Search may eventually include:

- articles
- guides
- videos
- services
- products
- market-intelligence content

Public availability remains governed by item status.

---

## 7. DO NOT

Do not create AI-generated search answers that appear factual without sourcing/architecture supporting them.

Do not expose inactive CONDITION as an available service through search.
