# AC Spatial Specialist — HOME Page Specification

Status: DRAFT
Page ID: HOME
Document Class: Public Page Specification
Intended Canonical Location: docs/30-design/pages/HOME.md

---

## 1. Purpose

HOME introduces AC Spatial Specialist and establishes the primary idea that property can be understood through different perspectives.

The page should quickly communicate:

WHO AC IS
WHAT AC DOES
WHY THE PERSPECTIVE MATTERS
WHERE THE USER CAN GO NEXT

---

## 2. Audience

Primary:

- property buyers
- property sellers
- property owners
- people evaluating property or space
- prospective SPACE clients

Secondary:

- content readers
- future market-intelligence users
- people discovering AC through social/search

---

## 3. Primary User Question

"What can AC help me understand or do?"

---

## 4. Primary Action

Explore active service perspectives.

DEAL

SPACE

---

## 5. Secondary Actions

Possible:

- learn about AC
- explore content
- contact AC
- use future tools

---

## 6. Information Hierarchy

1. AC identity
2. core value/perspective
3. DEAL / SPACE
4. methodology/context
5. useful content/tools
6. trust/person identity
7. contact

---

## 7. Hero

The hero should combine:

- strong brand statement
- Anh Cao / Owner identity where appropriate
- portrait
- primary CTA
- living visual environment

The hero should not become an information dashboard.

---

## 8. Explore Services

This is a major interaction.

Active perspectives:

DEAL
SPACE

CONDITION must remain hidden.

The existing successful Explore Services motion behavior should be preserved unless intentionally redesigned after owner review.

---

## 9. Visual Identity

CINEMATIC PROPERTY INTELLIGENCE

Suggested environment:

AC Ambient Intelligence

Possible elements:

- architectural grid
- dark spatial field
- subtle technical geometry
- controlled glow
- integrated portrait
- strong negative space

---

## 10. Motion

Global motion should remain slow.

Service exploration may have stronger interaction response.

Avoid constant competing animations.

---

## 11. Media

Potential:

- owner portrait
- owner-approved hero background
- procedural scene
- Story Backdrop
- selected editorial/property media

Owner-published media remains authoritative.

---

## 12. Responsive

Desktop:

cinematic / asymmetric composition.

Mobile:

strong vertical narrative.

Portrait/media must not overwhelm text or CTA.

---

## 13. Compliance

Do not present CONDITION.

Do not invent:

- credentials
- transactions
- testimonials
- market statistics
- awards

---

## 14. Owner Controls

Eventually Studio should manage:

- hero copy
- hero media
- service emphasis
- procedural scene
- page motion
- CTA
- featured content
- section visibility/order where supported

---

## 15. DO NOT

Do not make HOME:

- generic corporate landing page
- giant collection of cards
- fake market dashboard
- automatic catalog of every future feature
- excessively animated
