## 1. Purpose

Provide a structured archive of AC video content.

---

## 2. Identity

EDITORIAL VIDEO ARCHIVE

Not:

generic YouTube clone.

---

## 3. Potential Structure

FEATURED

LATEST

TOPICS

SERVICE RELATIONSHIPS

ARCHIVE

Only use sections supported by actual available content.

---

## 4. Media

Use:

- thumbnails
- posters
- metadata
- related content

Do not autoplay an archive full of videos.

---

## 5. Visual Identity

Cyber Archive.

Dark editorial environment with subtle metadata/data language.

Content remains dominant.

---

## 6. Interaction

Useful:

- filtering
- topic discovery
- service relationship
- article/guide relationships

Avoid unnecessary hover tricks that hide titles.

---

## 7. Empty / Early Stage

If content volume is small, present the available material honestly.

Do not fabricate categories or counts to make the archive appear larger.

---

## 8. Mobile

Prioritize thumbnail, title, topic, date/context and clear tap target.

---

## 9. DO NOT

Do not display fake view counts, popularity rankings or fabricated metadata.
