# AC Spatial Specialist — ABOUT Page Specification

Status: DRAFT
Page ID: ABOUT
Document Class: Public Page Specification
Intended Canonical Location: docs/30-design/pages/ABOUT.md

---

## 1. Purpose

Explain:

PERSON
+
PERSPECTIVE
+
SYSTEM

The page should introduce the person behind AC while showing that AC is being developed as a structured company/platform rather than merely a personality profile.

---

## 2. User Questions

Who is behind AC?

How does this person think about property and space?

What is AC trying to build?

Why does this perspective exist?

---

## 3. Primary Action

Continue into a relevant AC perspective:

DEAL or SPACE.

---

## 4. Content Architecture

Suggested sequence:

1. owner introduction
2. perspective/philosophy
3. relationship to property
4. relationship to space
5. relationship to learning/technology
6. AC system
7. next action

---

## 5. Visual Identity

HUMAN + DIGITAL ARCHITECTURE

Use:

- portrait
- quiet technical geometry
- environmental depth
- restrained halo
- editorial composition

Human subject remains primary.

---

## 6. Media

Canonical About portrait should use the Page Asset / Media system.

Do not depend exclusively on homepage media.

Safe fallback required.

---

## 7. Claims

Do not invent biography, credentials, achievements, client results, history or authority.

Only verified information may be presented as fact.

---

## 8. Responsive

Desktop may use asymmetric portrait/editorial layout.

Mobile should become a clean personal narrative.

---

## 9. DO NOT

Do not turn About into:

- résumé dump
- exaggerated founder mythology
- motivational manifesto with no business meaning
- technical system diagram that overshadows the human story
