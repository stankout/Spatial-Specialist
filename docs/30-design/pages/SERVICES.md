## 3. Primary User Question

"Which AC perspective is appropriate for my situation?"

---

## 4. Presentation

Avoid a generic SaaS pricing/feature-card grid.

Prefer:

PERSPECTIVE
→ QUESTION
→ PURPOSE
→ USER
→ NEXT ACTION

DEAL and SPACE should feel related but distinct.

---

## 5. DEAL

Core question:

How should I approach this property as a real-estate decision?

---

## 6. SPACE

Core question:

How should I understand this property or environment as a physical/spatial system?

---

## 7. Bundling

Do not imply that DEAL requires SPACE or SPACE requires DEAL.

Services may complement each other but remain distinct.

---

## 8. Visual Identity

Two connected perspectives within the same AC system.

DEAL:
strategic / market / directional.

SPACE:
spatial / orientation / relational.

---

## 9. CTA

Each active service gets its own clear path.

Avoid one ambiguous "Get Started" button for every service.

---

## 10. DO NOT

Do not show CONDITION as:

- available
- coming soon marketing CTA
- bookable
- hidden upsell
