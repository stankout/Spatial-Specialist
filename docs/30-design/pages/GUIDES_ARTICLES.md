## 2. Identity

structured

discoverable
connected to AC domains

## 3. Potential Content Types

ARTICLE

GUIDE

## 4. Archive Structure

Potential:

FEATURED

LATEST

TOPIC

DEAL

SPACE

MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Only expose categories supported by actual content.

---

## 5. Article Presentation

Prioritize:

title
summary
topic
publication/update date
meaningful media
reading content
related material

Avoid excessive decoration during long-form reading.

---

## 6. Visual Identity

Cyber Archive / Global Intelligence.

Archive pages may use richer environment.

Reading pages should become calmer.

---

## 7. Content Integrity

Distinguish:

facts
sources
interpretation
traditional frameworks
opinion

where relevant.

---

## 8. Related Content

Related content may connect:

Article
↔ Guide
↔ Video
↔ Service
↔ future Product

Relationships should be meaningful rather than automatic keyword spam.

---

## 9. DO NOT

Do not create fake publication volume, fake authors, fake citations or fake engagement.
