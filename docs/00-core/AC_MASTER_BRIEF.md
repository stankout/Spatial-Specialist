## 1. Identity

AC Spatial Specialist is a property-focused business and digital platform built around multiple perspectives for understanding, selecting, using, and managing real property and physical space.

The public brand is:

AC Spatial Specialist

The platform currently organizes its primary service perspectives as:

- DEAL — Real Estate
- SPACE — Spatial Consultation
- CONDITION — Home Inspection / Property Condition, currently inactive

AC is intended to grow beyond a traditional brochure website into a business, content, market-intelligence, service, and customer platform.

---

## 2. Purpose

AC exists to help people make better decisions about property and space by combining:

- real estate transaction knowledge
- property context
- spatial analysis
- educational content
- market information
- technology
- structured decision-making

The company should clearly distinguish factual information, professional services, interpretation, opinion, and future experimental products.

---

## 3. Core Principle

AC should prefer truthful, explainable information over unsupported marketing claims.

The company should not present:

- invented credentials
- invented transactions
- fabricated statistics
- fake testimonials
- unsupported market predictions
- inactive services as active services
- reference material as original production material

Technology should help explain decisions, not manufacture authority.

The repository is intended to become the durable operating memory of AC Spatial Specialist.

Knowledge authority should follow this principle:

DOMAIN SPEC governs.

FEATURE SPEC specifies.

ROADMAP plans.

CURRENT STATE reports.

AI HANDOFF orients.

---

## 8. Publication Principle

The platform distinguishes Draft state from Published state.

Draft content or configuration must not automatically become public.

Published state represents approved public configuration.

Owner-approved publication and applicable role/capability controls must remain respected.

---

## 9. Media Principle

Media must distinguish:

- approved production media
- owner media
- generated/original AC media
- external references
- unapproved imports

Reference assets exist for research and inspiration.

Reference material must not automatically become public production material.

AC-native visual production should favor:

- original assets
- owner-approved assets
- procedural graphics
- appropriately licensed media

---

## 10. Data Principle

Future market-intelligence products should distinguish:

RAW DATA

from

DERIVED SIGNALS

from

AC INTERPRETATION.

Where practical, market information should expose:

- source
- observation date
- refresh date
- methodology
- geographic scope

Derived indicators should be explainable rather than presented as mysterious AI scores.

- company direction
- brand positioning

- public claims

- credential-sensitive services

- production activation

Software permissions should support company authority rather than redefine it.

3. Draft state is not Published state.

4. Reference media is not production media.

5. Platform capability does not imply public service availability.

6. Business truth is defined by canonical business documentation, not inferred from software behavior.
