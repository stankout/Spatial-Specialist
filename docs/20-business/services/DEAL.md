## 1. Definition

DEAL is AC Spatial Specialist's real-estate service perspective.

It covers property decisions involving the transaction, market, location, negotiation, selection, sale, purchase, and related decision context within the verified professional authority of the provider.

DEAL is not simply a website category.

It represents an actual business/service domain.

---

## 2. Purpose

DEAL exists to help users move from:

PROPERTY QUESTION
→ CONTEXT
→ OPTIONS
→ COMPARISON
→ DECISION
→ TRANSACTION ACTION

with clearer information and structured reasoning.

---

## 3. Intended Audiences

Potential DEAL audiences include:

- home buyers
- home sellers
- property owners
- investors
- people evaluating a property or location
- people preparing for a future real-estate transaction
- users learning about the transaction process

Specific eligibility and representation depend on the applicable professional/business relationship.

---

## 5. DEAL Method

A general DEAL decision process may follow:

### 1. Context

Understand:

- user objective
- property type
- location
- timeline
- relevant constraints

### 2. Market

Observe available market information relevant to the decision.

### 3. Property

Evaluate the property within the scope appropriate to DEAL.

### 4. Options

Identify practical alternatives.

### 5. Tradeoffs

Explain what may be gained or sacrificed through each path.

### 6. Decision

Help organize the next reasonable action.

## 6. Relationship to Market Intelligence

DEAL may use AC market-intelligence products as decision-support tools.

Examples may eventually include:

- AC Market Pulse
- mortgage-rate context
- inventory trends
- price-reduction behavior
- days on market
- market comparisons
- affordability tools

A data product does not replace the professional service relationship.

Market indicators should support context rather than manufacture certainty.

---

## 7. Relationship to SPACE

A DEAL client may separately choose SPACE.

Examples:

A buyer may use DEAL for transaction/property selection and SPACE for spatial interpretation.

These perspectives may inform one another, but they should remain identifiable as distinct services.

Do not imply that a customer must purchase SPACE to receive DEAL.

---

## 8. Relationship to CONDITION

CONDITION is currently INACTIVE.

DEAL must not present AC CONDITION/Home Inspection as an available AC service while CONDITION remains inactive.

Where property-condition expertise outside DEAL is needed, communication must reflect the actual currently available professional/service options rather than imply an inactive internal capability is available.

A successful DEAL experience should leave the user with greater clarity regarding:

- the transaction
- available options
- important tradeoffs
- relevant market context
