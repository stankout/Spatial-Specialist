## 1. Definition

SPACE is AC Spatial Specialist's spatial-consultation perspective.

It focuses on relationships between:

- people
- property
- orientation
- circulation
- layout
- environment
- placement
- use of space
- spatial context

SPACE may incorporate Feng Shui where requested, but SPACE is not limited to Feng Shui.

---

## 2. Purpose

SPACE exists to help clients see and understand physical environments through structured spatial observation and interpretation.

A general progression is:

OBSERVE
→ ORIENT
→ RELATE
→ INTERPRET
→ RECOMMEND

## 4. SPACE Method

SPACE may use a structured method such as:

### CONTEXT

Who uses the space?

What is the space intended to support?

What constraints exist?

### ORIENTATION

How is the property/building/room oriented?

What are the relevant directional relationships?

### CIRCULATION

How do people, attention, activity, and access move through the environment?

### LAYOUT

How are important physical elements related?

### ENVIRONMENT

What relevant external/internal conditions can be observed?

### INTERPRETATION

What patterns or implications may follow from the selected analytical framework?

### RECOMMENDATIONS

What practical changes, considerations, or options may be useful?

## 5. Feng Shui

Feng Shui may be used within SPACE as a traditional spatial interpretive framework.

When used, AC should distinguish:

OBSERVABLE CONDITION

from:

TRADITIONAL / INTERPRETIVE MEANING.

Example conceptual distinction:

Observation:
A large tree directly occupies the approach to the main entrance.

Interpretation:
A selected Feng Shui framework may interpret this relationship in a particular way.

Practical consideration:
The tree may also affect visibility, access, maintenance, root interaction, light, or perception depending on the actual property.

Do not automatically merge these three categories into one claim.

## 7. Relationship to DEAL

DEAL answers primarily:

What does this property mean in the context of a real-estate decision?

SPACE answers primarily:

What does this property or environment mean from a spatial perspective?

A client may use either independently.

They may also use both when appropriate.

Do not present the services as obligatorily bundled.

---

## 8. Relationship to CONDITION

SPACE does not become a property inspection service merely because visible physical conditions are discussed.

SPACE may observe visible conditions as contextual information.

It must not present CONDITION/Home Inspection as an active AC service while CONDITION remains inactive.

---

## 9. Recommendation Standard

SPACE recommendations should distinguish between:

- direct physical observation
- practical spatial suggestion
- interpretive framework
- personal preference
- matters requiring another qualified professional

Where uncertainty exists, say so.

## 13. Claims Boundary

SPACE should not imply professional authority in fields outside its actual verified qualifications.

Examples may include:

- architecture
- engineering
- structural assessment
- environmental testing
- medical advice
- legal advice
- appraisal
- surveying
- financial advice
- regulated inspection services

where AC has not separately established the necessary authority.

---

## 14. SPACE Success

A successful SPACE engagement should help the client better understand:

- how the environment is organized
- what relationships matter
- what may be creating friction
- which interpretations are being used
- what practical changes or considerations are available

The purpose is not to manufacture certainty from subjective interpretation.
