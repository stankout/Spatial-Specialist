# AC Spatial Specialist — Customer Lifecycle

Status: DRAFT
Document Class: Business Operations Specification
Intended Canonical Location: docs/20-business/CUSTOMER_LIFECYCLE.md

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the general lifecycle from first awareness through an ongoing AC relationship.

Individual services may define additional service-specific stages.

---

## 2. Lifecycle

AWARENESS
→ INTEREST
→ LEAD
→ QUALIFICATION
→ SERVICE / PRODUCT PATH
→ ENGAGEMENT
→ DELIVERY
→ FOLLOW-UP
→ RETENTION / REFERRAL

---

## 3. Awareness

A person may discover AC through:

- website
- search
- social media
- video
- article
- advertising
- referral
- offline interaction

At this stage the person is not automatically a Lead.

---

## 4. Interest

The person interacts with AC information or expresses interest.

Examples:

- viewing a service
- using a future tool
- reading content
- exploring a booking path

Tracking should respect applicable privacy and consent requirements.

---

## 5. Lead

A person becomes a Lead when they enter an identifiable AC inquiry/acquisition workflow.

Potential sources:

- contact form
- booking request
- direct inquiry
- referral
- service inquiry
- campaign response

---

## 6. Qualification

Determine:

- objective
- requested service
- location
- timeline
- relevant constraints
- service fit
- next action

Qualification should not collect unnecessary information.

---

## 7. Service / Product Path

Route the person appropriately.

Examples:

DEAL
SPACE
future CONDITION
content/resource
product
future Market Intelligence tool

Do not force every user into a sales path.

---

## 8. Engagement

Where a formal service relationship begins, establish relevant expectations.

Depending on service this may include:

- scope
- agreement
- appointment
- required information
- payment arrangement
- representation/business relationship
- deliverable expectations

---

## 9. Delivery

Perform the approved service/product process.

Service-specific delivery belongs in the relevant service SOP.

---

## 10. Follow-Up

After delivery:

- confirm completion
- answer appropriate follow-up
- provide next steps
- record unresolved issues
- update customer record where appropriate

---

## 11. Retention

Where useful and appropriate:

- continued educational content
- future services
- tools
- relevant updates
- relationship maintenance

Do not create artificial recurring communication without appropriate consent.

---

## 12. Referral

Satisfied clients/customers may choose to refer others.

AC should not fabricate, pressure, or manipulate referrals or reviews.

---

## 13. Lifecycle Record

Operational systems may eventually record:

- source
- stage
- service interest
- owner
- last activity
- next action
- consent/preferences
- relevant service relationship

Do not place sensitive information in general notes where a more appropriate protected record exists.
