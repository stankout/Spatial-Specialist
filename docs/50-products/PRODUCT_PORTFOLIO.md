## 1. Purpose

This document identifies product concepts within the AC ecosystem.

A Product is distinct from:

- a professional Service
- a website Feature
- Content

Products require their own specification and lifecycle before becoming ACTIVE.

---

## 2. Product Lifecycle

IDEA

Early concept.

PROPOSED

Defined sufficiently for evaluation.

APPROVED

Owner-approved for development or release planning.

BUILDING

Implementation in progress.

ACTIVE

Approved and available to intended users.

BLOCKED

Approved direction exists but progress is prevented by a dependency.

RETIRED

No longer actively offered.

# 5. Product Activation Principle

A product does not become ACTIVE because:

- code exists
- a mockup exists
- it appears in the roadmap
- a prototype exists

Normal path:

IDEA
→ PROPOSAL
→ SPECIFICATION
→ OWNER APPROVAL
→ BUILD
→ QA
→ ACTIVATION

---

# 6. Product Truth

Every significant approved product should eventually have one canonical Product Specification.

Product configuration and software implementation should reference that specification rather than invent product behavior independently.
